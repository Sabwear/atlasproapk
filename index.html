Device-Specific Installation Guides

Select your platform below and follow the step-by-step instructions. Most TV setups take under five minutes using the free Downloader app.

1. Android TV, Google TV & Android Box

Works on Chromecast with Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Xiaomi Mi Box / TV Stick, Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense, and standard Android TV boxes.

  1. Install the Downloader app: Open the Google Play Store on your TV or Android box, search for Downloader by AFTVnews, and install it.
  2. Allow unknown apps for Downloader: Open Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions (or Privacy → Security & restrictions on Google TV), select Unknown sources, and toggle Downloader to On.
  3. Enter the official code or URL: Launch Downloader, accept the storage permission prompt, select the URL field on the Home screen, type 765432 (or the direct official APK URL), and select Go.
  4. Install the APK: Once the download finishes, the Android package installer opens automatically. Select Install and wait for confirmation.
  5. Clean up and launch: Choose Done, delete the temporary APK file when Downloader prompts you to free up storage, then open Atlas Pro ONTV from your Apps list and sign in with your existing credentials.

2. Amazon Firestick & Fire TV

Compatible with Fire TV Stick Lite, Fire TV Stick HD, Fire TV Stick 4K / 4K Max, and Fire TV Cube running Fire OS.

  1. Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore: From the Fire TV Home screen, open Find → Search, type Downloader, choose the orange Downloader by AFTVnews app, and select Download.
  2. Unlock Developer Options (Fire OS 7/8): Go to Settings → My Fire TV → About. Highlight your device name (e.g., Fire TV Stick 4K) and press the center Select button 7 times until you see "No need, you are already a developer."
  3. Enable Install Unknown Apps: Press Back once to open Developer Options → Install unknown apps, then turn Downloader to ON.
  4. Download the official APK: Open Downloader, grant file access, enter the code 765432 in the address bar, and click Go.
  5. Complete installation: When prompted, select Install. After installation finishes, select Done, click Delete twice to remove the installer file, and launch Atlas Pro ONTV from Your Apps & Channels.

3. Android Smartphones & Tablets

Compatible with phones and tablets running Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or newer.

  1. Download from the official provider: Open Chrome or your preferred mobile browser and visit the official Atlas Pro ONTV provider page to download the latest .apk file.
  2. Confirm the browser download: If Android displays a standard warning that APK files downloaded outside Google Play can be harmful, tap Download anyway only after verifying you are on the official provider URL.
  3. Allow installation from your browser: Tap the downloaded file in your notification shade or Files → Downloads folder. If prompted, tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source for your browser.
  4. Install and open: Tap Install, wait a few seconds, and tap Open. Enter your existing account code or login details to load your profile.

4. Smart TVs (Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Compatibility Notes)

Important compatibility note: Samsung Smart TVs run Tizen OS and LG Smart TVs run webOS. Neither operating system can open or install Android .apk files natively, so the Atlas Pro ONTV APK cannot be sideloaded directly onto those TVs.

  1. Option A — Use a compatible TV store player: Open the Samsung Smart Hub or LG Content Store and install a standard playlist player supported by your TV model (such as IBO Player, SmartOne, or Set IPTV), then configure it using the playlist details provided by your authorized service.
  2. Option B — Connect an external streaming stick (recommended for the native app): Plug an Amazon Fire TV Stick, Chromecast with Google TV, or Android TV Box into an open HDMI port on your Samsung or LG TV, and follow the Firestick or Android TV steps above.
Security reminder: Be cautious of websites claiming to offer a "Samsung Tizen APK" or "LG webOS APK." Those file formats do not exist for Samsung or LG TVs.

Troubleshooting Common Installation Errors

Practical fixes for the most frequent Android, Android TV, and Fire OS sideloading issues.

"Install from Unknown Sources" Is Blocked or Missing

  • Android 8.0 and newer: There is no global Unknown Sources toggle. Grant permission per app under Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and enable your browser or Downloader.
  • Fire TV / Firestick: If Developer Options is hidden, go to Settings → My Fire TV → About and click your device name 7 times rapidly to reveal it.
  • Managed or kid profiles: Switch to the primary administrator profile; restricted profiles block sideloading by design.

"App Not Installed" or Parsing Package Error

  • Incomplete download: Delete the partial .apk file, verify your internet connection is stable, and re-download the package.
  • Low free storage: Android requires extra headroom to unpack an APK. Ensure at least 500 MB of free internal storage is available.
  • Signature conflict: If an older or modified build is already installed, uninstall the previous app completely before installing the official APK.
  • OS incompatibility: Confirm your device runs Android 5.0 or higher and matches the CPU architecture (most TV sticks use 32-bit ARM).

Updating from an Older Version

  • Save your login details first: Write down your activation code, username, or playlist details before making changes.
  • Clean reinstall if update fails: If installing the new APK over the old one fails, go to Settings → Apps → Atlas Pro ONTV, choose Force Stop, then Uninstall.
  • Install the latest build: Download the current version via the official link or Downloader code 765432, install it, and re-enter your saved credentials.

App Won't Open or Crashes After Install

  • Clear app cache: Navigate to Settings → Apps → Atlas Pro ONTV → Clear cache (avoid Clear data unless you want to re-enter your login).
  • Restart the device: Unplug your Firestick or Android box for 30 seconds to clear memory locks, then power it back on.
  • Check date & time: Incorrect system clocks can break HTTPS connections. Enable Automatic date & time in system settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about app safety, updates, compatibility, and account requirements.

Is the Atlas Pro ONTV APK safe to install?

The official APK file is safe when obtained directly from the official Atlas Pro ONTV provider or through the verified Downloader code. Avoid third-party mirror sites that repackage or modify APK files, and keep Google Play Protect enabled to scan downloads.

How do I get official updates for Atlas Pro ONTV?

Because the app is sideloaded outside the Google Play Store, it does not auto-update through Play Services. Check the official provider page or re-enter the current Downloader code whenever a new release is announced, then install the updated APK over or in place of the older build.

Which devices are compatible with the Atlas Pro ONTV app?

The native APK runs on Android 5.0 and newer, including Android TV, Google TV (such as Chromecast with Google TV and NVIDIA Shield), Android TV boxes, Amazon Firestick and Fire TV devices, and Android smartphones and tablets.

Does the app include channels, or is an existing account required?

Atlas Pro ONTV is strictly a media player application and does not include built-in channels or media streams. You must already have an active account or activation code from your own authorized provider to sign in. This website does not sell subscriptions or accounts.

Why is Atlas Pro ONTV not listed on the Google Play Store or Amazon Appstore?

Many independent IPTV and media player apps are distributed directly as APK packages by their developers rather than through app store marketplaces. Sideloading via a browser or the Downloader app is the standard installation method on Android TV and Fire OS.

Can I install the Atlas Pro ONTV APK directly on a Samsung or LG Smart TV?

No. Samsung Smart TVs run Tizen OS and LG Smart TVs run webOS, neither of which supports Android .apk files. To use your playlist on those TVs, either install a compatible player from the Samsung or LG app store or connect an external Android TV / Fire TV streaming stick via HDMI.
